At Pyramid Paints, we are more than just a paint manufacturer — we are pioneers, trendsetters, and evolution companions. Our story began as a regional coatings supplier in Pune.

Today, we develop innovative coating systems for industries that move: Automotive, Electronics, Lifestyle, Construction, and Glass. Our strength? The courage to break new ground and the curiosity to think differently.

As a partner and supplier of unique visual and tactile coating effects, we make it our mission — from the very first second — to give products a distinctive character that enhances desirability and offers real added value.

Waterborne Coatings

UV Coatings
