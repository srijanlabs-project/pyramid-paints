INDUSTRIES
Sectors We Serve

From cars to
circuit boards.

We formulate coatings that solve real industrial problems — adhesion challenges, harsh environments, extreme temperatures, regulatory compliance. Wherever surfaces need protection or enhancement, we have a solution.

01
Automotive
OEM · Tier-1 · Refinish
Topcoats & Basecoats
High-solid PU and water-based basecoat/clearcoat systems for passenger vehicles. Colour consistency, UV resistance, and hardness meeting OEM specifications.
Underbody & Cavity
Anti-corrosion underbody coatings and wax-based cavity protection. Designed for Indian road conditions — extreme heat, monsoon moisture, gravel impact.
Plastic Parts
Flexible primers and topcoats for bumpers, fascias, and interior trims. Superior adhesion to PP, ABS, and TPO without surface treatment where possible.
Refinish Systems
Complete refinish systems for repair shops — matching OEM gloss levels, easy spray application, fast flash times to minimise booth time.
02
Electronics
PCB · Displays · Enclosures
Conformal Coatings
Acrylic, polyurethane, and silicone conformal coatings for PCB assemblies. IPC-CC-830 compliant, selective coating ready, UV-traceable.
Thermal Management
High-emissivity thermal coatings for LED drivers, power modules, and battery enclosures. Reduces junction temperatures significantly.
Anti-Static Finishes
ESD-safe topcoats and primers for electronic enclosures and cleanroom environments. Surface resistance controlled to specification.
Display Coatings
Anti-glare and anti-reflective coatings for display glass and covers. Low haze, controlled gloss, compatible with touch and optical bonding processes.
03
Construction
Exterior · Interior · Flooring
Exterior Wall Coatings
Elastomeric and silicone-modified exterior paints for India's climate extremes. Crack-bridging, dirt-repellent, 8–10 year durability.
Waterproofing
Terrace, basement, and wet-area waterproofing systems. Crystalline, cementitious, and membrane-based options for different substrates.
Industrial Flooring
Epoxy and PU floor coating systems for warehouses, factories, and commercial kitchens. Seamless, chemical-resistant, anti-slip.
Interior Finishes
Premium interior emulsions and texture coatings for residential and commercial interiors. Low VOC, washable, and available in 600+ colours.
